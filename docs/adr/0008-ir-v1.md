# ADR 0008 — IR v1: the prototype graph

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21

## Context

The design plans one intermediate model (IR) for the four formats: pages, flows, frames, a render
tree, interactions, components and variants. Phase 1 only bakes `.fig` files, and Phase 0 showed
that rendering a `.fig` straight from its own tree is both simple and faithful (ADR 0001). Designing
a render tree that also fits Sketch, XD and Penpot now would be guesswork.

## Decision

**IR v1 holds the prototype graph only**, in `folioscope_core::ir`, serializable with serde
(camelCase JSON, tagged enums):

- `Document`: source format, pages.
- `Page`: name, `flows` (name, description, starting frame), `frames`.
- `Frame`: id, name, bounds on the canvas, `hotspots`, and its `overlay` settings when it opens as
  one: position (centered, a corner or side, or `manual` at the opening action's `offset` from
  the hotspot), backdrop color, close on click outside; and its `scroll` when its content goes past
  its bounds: direction, content size, ids of the fixed children. Hotspots in a fixed child are
  `fixed`; a "scroll to" carries its target's bounds and its offset.
- `Hotspot`: the node that carries interactions, with its **bounds in frame coordinates**
  (bounding box of the node after its transforms), and its `interactions`. A frame's own
  interactions (e.g. "after delay") use a hotspot covering the frame.
- `Interaction`: a `Trigger` (click, hover, press, mouse enter/leave, after delay, drag, key,
  other) and an `Action` (navigate, overlay, swap overlay, scroll to, back, close, open URL,
  unsupported).
- `Transition`: kind (instant, dissolve, smart animate, slide/push/move in or out from a side…),
  duration in milliseconds, easing (named or cubic Bézier).

Ids are strings: each format keeps its own (`12:34` for Figma, UUIDs elsewhere). The schema
version is `IR_VERSION`, now 1, written into every `.folio`.

Rendering stays format-specific (`folioscope_bake::svg` reads the `.fig` tree). The render tree
joins the IR, as IR v2, when a second format is rendered (Phase 2, Sketch).

## Consequences

- `.fig` → IR exists in Phase 1; Sketch, XD and Penpot keep their summaries until Phase 2 maps
  them onto the IR (their format notes show they are subsets of the same graph).
- Instances are walked through their component's children, with their overrides resolved
  (`figma::instance`): hotspots inside them count, at their positions after auto layout.
- An instance whose variant holds a "change to" becomes a `Component` of its frame: an id (the
  instances from the frame down to it), its bounds, its first variant, and one state per
  variant reachable through "change to", each with its own hotspots. At most 16 states per
  component (D7); nested interactive components inside a state are drawn, not interactive.
- The `.folio` `index.json` is the IR plus, per frame, its baked layers (ADR 0004).
