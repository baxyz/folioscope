# Compatibility

What the player does with each design format today. Hover or focus a symbol for its details. The
formats do not all have the same features: Sketch only has click links, for instance, hence the
dashes. What is left to do, and how to help, is on the [Roadmap](/roadmap).

<FeatureMatrix />

A `.fig` file stores the shape of every letter: its text needs no font. For the other formats,
give the [fonts](/guide/fonts) the design uses.
