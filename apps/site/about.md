# About

**Folioscope** plays design prototypes on your own site. It is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3.0 or later](https://github.com/baxyz/folioscope/blob/main/LICENSE). The design files you play remain yours: the licence covers the player, not your mockups.

## Not affiliated

Folioscope is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Figma, Sketch, Adobe or Penpot. Their names are used only to describe file compatibility.

Folioscope builds on [openfig-core](https://github.com/OpenFig-org/openfig-core) (MIT), [Sketch's file format](https://github.com/sketch-hq/sketch-file-format) (MIT), [Penpot](https://penpot.app) (MPL-2.0), [resvg](https://github.com/linebender/resvg) and many other open-source crates and packages, credited in [NOTICE](https://github.com/baxyz/folioscope/blob/main/NOTICE).

No tracking: this site sets no cookie and runs no analytics.
