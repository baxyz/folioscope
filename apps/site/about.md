# About

**Folioscope** plays design prototypes on your own site. It is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3.0 or later](https://github.com/baxyz/folioscope/blob/main/LICENSE). The design files you play remain yours: the licence covers the player, not your mockups.

## Why it exists

Folioscope started from a portfolio: keeping one's own design work alive on it, for years. Design
tools close, change their file formats, move features behind new plans or change their terms, and
a prototype that only plays inside them can disappear with them. Folioscope bakes the prototype
into plain files that live on your own site: they keep playing whatever happens to the tool that
made them. It is a way to preserve your own creations, not only to show them.
